The "Hidden" Standard Deviations: Why 0.5 & 1.5 SD Bands Matter

Most discussions around VWAP standard deviations focus on the 1.0 and 2.0 bands, often treating them as simple overbought/oversold levels. However, institutional algorithms operate with far greater precision. The 0.5 and 1.5 SD bands are where the subtle, yet powerful, shifts in market dynamics occur. The 0.5 SD band often acts as a magnetic zone for price during strong trends, serving as a re-accumulation or distribution point for large orders. The 1.5 SD band, on the other hand, is a highly reactive zone, signaling potential exhaustion and aggressive mean-reversion opportunities before the market reaches extreme 2.0 SD levels.
