## CFTC v. Traders Global Group / MyForexFunds

- CFTC complaint (filed August 28, 2023): https://www.cftc.gov/media/9196/enftradersglobalgroupcomplaint082923/download
- District of New Jersey preliminary-injunction opinion, November 14, 2023: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/new-jersey/njdce/3:2023cv11808/523880/134/
- Special Master's report/recommendation (filed May 13, 2025; recommendation was not itself the final district-court order): https://www.cftc.gov/media/12106/ogc_KazmiReportRecommendationSactions051325/download
- District Court's May 13, 2025 order, reproducing Order No. 260: https://complianceconcourse.willkie.com/app/uploads/2025/06/20250513-CFTC-Traders-Global-Order.pdf

The 2023 complaint made allegations. The November 2023 order granted some preliminary relief under a prima-facie standard after an evidentiary hearing, not a final merits adjudication. The May 13, 2025 order adopted the Special Master's recommendation, dismissed the complaint with prejudice, denied the separate dismissal motion as moot, and directed a fee/cost affidavit for the sanctions motion. The termination is case-specific and does not decide the legality of every simulated-account or proprietary-trading program.

## VWAP education and opening-range schedule

- BIS 2025 Triennial Survey (FX / OTC market survey context): https://www.bis.org/publications/triennial-central-bank-survey-foreign-exchange-and-over-the-counter-otc-derivatives-markets-2025
- TradingView VWAP definition/calculation/bands inputs: https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000502018-volume-weighted-average-price-vwap/
- NYSE hours and holiday calendar: https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars

TradingView describes VWAP as a volume-weighted average of cumulative price-volume over cumulative volume; its standard-deviation mode calculates standard deviations of VWAP values since the anchor, while percentage mode uses percentage bands. The implementation and settings matter. The BIS 2025 survey is an official OTC FX market survey; its existence helps anchor the article’s distinction between OTC FX data and a single listed equity venue. No source substantiates claims that particular deviations are institutional entry triggers or guarantee reversals. NYSE specifies the core session 9:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. ET; that schedule is venue-specific and subject to holidays/early closes. Forex session labels should not be represented as universal fixed GMT times or guaranteed profit windows.
